# Birkat Kohanim poster: sources and decisions

## The piece (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

Birkat Kohanim spiralling inward, with a hamsa in the middle.

## Purpose (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

For sale, as a print or framed poster. So every text and font must allow
commercial use: no CC-BY-SA text, no Meir Sadan fonts without his
permission.

## Text

- Source: Westminster Leningrad Codex, Sefaria "Tanach with Ta'amei
  Hamikra" (public domain), from the public Sefaria export
  (`json/Tanakh/Torah/Numbers/Hebrew/Tanach with Ta'amei Hamikra.json`).
- `text.lua` is generated by `make-text.py` from the export: 15 words, 60
  letters (6:24: 3 words, 15 letters; 6:25: 5, 20; 6:26: 7, 25). The maqaf,
  paseq, sof pasuq and (ס) are dropped.
- **The Name (Yosef, 2026-11-10):** written in full, the four letters, in
  all three places. In `text.lua` it is the token NAME; `spiral.lua`
  assembles it from code points, so it is never written out in any file.
- **Verses (Yosef, 2026-11-10):** the blessing only, Numbers 6:24-26,
  without 6:23 or 6:27.
- **Letters (Yosef, 2026-11-10):** unpointed (no nikud, no te'amim), in
  Taamey David CLM (`shared/fonts/`, Culmus Project, GPL with font
  exception: fine for sale). The points, te'amim, paseq and sof pasuq are
  stripped from the source text by code point.
- Still to decide (Yosef): checking the text against his siddur.

## Spiral direction and start (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

The spiral starts with יברכך and runs clockwise and inward: down and to
the left along the bottom. So that the Hebrew reads right to left, the
letters' tops face the centre (upright along the bottom, upside down
across the top). Yosef first had it start in the bottom right quadrant,
then moved it: the yod of יברכך sits just below the centre on the right.
`start` = -10 degrees: the yod's centre is 10 degrees below the
horizontal through the spiral's centre.

## Letter size (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

The letters shrink as the spiral runs inward (Yosef first chose one size
throughout, then a little shrinking, then a bit more): the innermost
letter is 0.7 of the outermost (`shrink`), the size falling in proportion
to the radius; at poster size 114.7 pt outside to 80.7 pt inside. The
turns are 2 letter bodies apart at the current size (`lead`), so they
close up a little as the letters shrink. About two and a half turns.

## Between verses (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

No mark: a single word space, so the blessing runs as one line. Yosef
tried a small flower, then a dot, and liked neither.

## The hamsa (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

Yosef sent four photos of hamsas he likes (for their style; none is
copied). What they share, and what the drawing now follows: the
traditional shape, three rounded fingers side by side with the middle one
tallest, two thumbs curling outward, a round palm; drawn as one outline
with a line between each pair of fingers (`hamsa_right()` in
`spiral.lua`, built from arcs and curves, no clipping). Yosef found it too squat
twice: it is now about 1.65 times as tall as wide (slimmer fingers, and
the whole drawn at 0.82 of its designed width, `hamsa_width`, with the
circle and star kept round), and half its height is 0.135 of the width
(`hamsa`). Yosef then asked for a shorter palm and taller fingers at the
same height: the fingers now meet the palm at -0.08 of the half-height
(`hamsa_foot`; designed at 0.12), so the fingers are about 1.33 times as
long and the palm about 0.82 as tall. The fingertips, curls, circle and
star keep their shapes; only their positions move (`ymap`). Inside (Yosef): a Magen David in a circle in the
palm, with scrollwork around it. Yosef asked for fuller scrollwork, filling
the hand as in two of his photos. As drawn (`hamsa_inside()`, a proposal
in its details): the circle centred low in the palm, the star two
triangles reaching 0.82 of its radius; in the middle finger a stem with
two curls at each end; an S-scroll down each side finger; a curl in each
thumb; an S-scroll each side of the circle; a pair of curls above it and
below it. Plain lines, thinner than the outline
(`hamsa_detail`), the left side mirroring the right.

## Draft layout (proposals, not yet Yosef's decisions)

- Letters set one by one on the spiral, each turned to it (Hebrew in TikZ
  per letter: no joining, so nothing is lost).
- The whole drawing centred on the page by its ink: `glyph-bounds.lua`
  holds each letter's outline hull from the font (made by
  `tools/make_glyph_bounds.py` with fontTools), so turned letters are
  measured by their ink, not their boxes. Checked from a render: the
  margins agree to 0.3 mm.
- Formats aceo, siddur, birkon, poster (12x18 placeholder), set by
  `\birkatformat` at the top of `main.tex`; small grey draft label.
