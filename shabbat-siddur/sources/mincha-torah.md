## Editorial decisions

- Service only, no reading text (Yosef). The rubric names what is read (at
  least ten verses of next week's parashah) and that Half Kaddish is not said
  after it, as the source does.
- וַאֲנִי תְפִלָּתִי: marked "twice" in the source; printed twice in full.
- Berich Shemeh: the source's parenthesized phrase is printed in square
  brackets with a rubric (house style for optional words); the instruction to
  bow is a rubric.
- Psalm 92 and יְהַלְלוּ, set small in the source, are normal size.
