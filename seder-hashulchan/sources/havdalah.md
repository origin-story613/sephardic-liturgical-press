## Editorial decisions

- אָנָּא … הוֹשִׁיעָה נָּא and אָנָּא … הַצְלִיחָה נָא: the source marks each
  "twice"; each is printed twice in full.
- The spice blessing: the source gives עֲצֵי with עִשְׂבֵּי and מִינֵי in
  parentheses; set as three alternatives, each with an English rubric (as in
  Me'ein Shalosh).
- The rubric before the blessing over fire is our own summary of the source's
  instruction (curl the fingers, look at the nails).
- The blessing after wine is not repeated here; the rubric points to Me'ein
  Shalosh (shared text).
- Not included pending Yosef's decision: the source's "Before Havdalah"
  material (Eliyahu HaNavi, the בְּסִימָן טוֹב prayer, שַׁעֲרֵי אוֹרָה, and the
  prayers of R. Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev).
