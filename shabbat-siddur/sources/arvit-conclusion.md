# Motzei Shabbat Arvit: Vihi Noam to the end

Section file: `sections/arvit-conclusion.tex` (in `shabbat-siddur/`).

## Source

- **Text:** *Siddur Edot HaMizrach*, version "Torat Emet 357", as published by
  Sefaria (Sefaria-Export, October 2026): `Weekday Arvit/Amidah` 54–71; `Weekday Arvit/Alenu`.
- **Original source:** Torat Emet freeware (toratemetfreeware.com).
- **License:** **Public Domain** (per Sefaria's version metadata).

## Editorial decisions

- Order: Torat Emet through Psalm 121 (Half Kaddish, Vihi Noam and Psalm 91,
  VeAtah Kadosh, Kaddish Shalem, Psalm 121, Kaddish Yehei Shelama), then the
  ending Yosef confirmed: Aleinu, Barechu, Kaddish Yehei Shelama.
- VeAtah Kadosh: the Aramaic (marked "quietly" in the source) is in
  parentheses.
- The source's "until here, standing" becomes a rubric before Vihi Noam.
- **Not included:** the הֲרֵינִי prayer some say before Vihi Noam.

## Open (siddur photos)

- The outline had Kaddish Shalem before Vihi Noam and no Psalm 121; Torat Emet
  has Half Kaddish, then Kaddish Shalem after VeAtah Kadosh. Draft follows
  Torat Emet; Yosef had no preference; settle from the siddur.
