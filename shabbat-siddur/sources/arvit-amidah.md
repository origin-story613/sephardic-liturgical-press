## Editorial decisions

- Atah Honantanu is printed as part of the fourth blessing without a seasonal
  rubric, since this book is only for Motzei Shabbat.
- Seasonal insertions kept from the base text: Ten Days of Repentance,
  summer/winter (מוֹרִיד הַטָּל; בָּרְכֵנוּ / בָּרֵךְ עָלֵינוּ), Rosh Hodesh and Hol
  HaMoed, Hanukkah and Purim, Tisha B'Av (עֲנֵנוּ, and נַחֵם as the Jerusalem
  custom).
- The Name in blessing closings: as in the Mincha Amidah (standard pointing;
  to be decided).
- The weekday-only Kaddish Titkabal after the Amidah is not printed.
