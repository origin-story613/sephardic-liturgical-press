# Menorah Shiviti (Shiviti 1)

Psalm 67 typeset in the shape of a seven-branched menorah, with Psalm 16:8
and the Name above, in Stam Sefarad CLM, with LuaLaTeX and TikZ. This is
**Shiviti 1**: the version carried by the birkon, Seder HaShulchan and Shabbat
siddur. Shiviti 2 (`menorah-shiviti-2/`) began as a copy of it.

- `main.tex`: standalone output; choose `aceo`, `siddur`, `birkon` or
  `poster` with `\menorahformat`.
- `menorah-setup.tex`, `menorah.lua`, `psalm67.lua`: the drawing.
- `sources.md`: text, counts, licenses and every decision. `HANDOFF.md`: rules
  and progress.
