# Birkat Kohanim poster: sources and decisions

## The piece (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

Birkat Kohanim spiralling inward, with a hamsa in the middle.

## Purpose (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

For sale, as a print or framed poster. So every text and font must allow
commercial use: no CC-BY-SA text, no Meir Sadan fonts without his
permission.

## Text

- Source: Westminster Leningrad Codex, Sefaria "Tanach with Ta'amei
  Hamikra" (public domain), from the public Sefaria export
  (`json/Tanakh/Torah/Numbers/Hebrew/Tanach with Ta'amei Hamikra.json`).
- `text.lua` is generated by `make-text.py` from the export: 15 words, 60
  letters (6:24: 3 words, 15 letters; 6:25: 5, 20; 6:26: 7, 25). The maqaf,
  paseq, sof pasuq and (ס) are dropped.
- **The Name (Yosef, 2026-11-10):** written in full, the four letters, in
  all three places. In `text.lua` it is the token NAME; `spiral.lua`
  assembles it from code points, so it is never written out in any file.
- **Verses (Yosef, 2026-11-10):** the blessing only, Numbers 6:24-26,
  without 6:23 or 6:27.
- **Letters (Yosef, 2026-11-10):** unpointed (no nikud, no te'amim), in
  Taamey David CLM (`shared/fonts/`, Culmus Project, GPL with font
  exception: fine for sale). The points, te'amim, paseq and sof pasuq are
  stripped from the source text by code point.
- Still to decide (Yosef): checking the text against his siddur.

## Spiral direction (Yosef, 2026-11-10)

The spiral starts in the bottom right quadrant with יברכך and runs to the
left along the bottom, clockwise and inward. So that the Hebrew reads right
to left, the letters' tops face the centre (upright along the bottom,
upside down across the top). `start` = -45 degrees: the first word's
letters are centred from about -48 to -95 degrees, so it begins in the
bottom right quadrant and ends just past the bottom centre.

## First draft (proposals, not yet Yosef's decisions)

- One logarithmic spiral (direction above). Each
  letter's size is in proportion to its distance from the centre (about
  146 pt on the outside down to about 61 pt on the inside, at poster size),
  so the spiral is the same shape on every turn; about two turns.
- Letters set one by one on the spiral, each turned to it (Hebrew in TikZ
  per letter: no joining, so nothing is lost).
- Two word spaces between verses, no other mark.
- The whole drawing centred on the page by its ink.
- The hamsa: a placeholder outline, fingers up, empty inside.
- Formats aceo, siddur, birkon, poster (12x18 placeholder), set by
  `\birkatformat` at the top of `main.tex`; small grey draft label.
