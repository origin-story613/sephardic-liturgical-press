## Editorial decisions

- Tzidkatcha with the source's rule as a rubric (Yosef asked for the omission
  rubric): said on a Shabbat when Tahanun would be said at a weekday Mincha;
  otherwise יְהִי שֵׁם is said instead (source).
- Shabbat Shuvah: rubric that Avinu Malkenu is said; its text is not printed.
- Order follows Torat Emet: Kaddish Shalem, Psalm 111, Kaddish Yehei Shelama,
  Aleinu.
