## Editorial decisions

- The source's long note on the intention of the Shema is shortened to one
  English rubric. בָּרוּךְ שֵׁם כְּבוֹד מַלְכוּתוֹ is in parentheses (whispered).
- The notes to stress the zayin in וּזְכַרְתֶּם and תִּזְכְּרוּ are dropped.
- The chazzan's repetition of the closing words is under a rubric.
