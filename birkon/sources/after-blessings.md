# Me'ein Shalosh and Borei Nefashot: sources

Section file: `birkon/sections/after-blessings.tex`

## Source

Transcribed from Yosef's Turkish siddur, pp. 349–350 (Yosef's decision to
include the after-blessings; the blessings before food are left out). This is
traditional liturgical wording.

## Editorial decisions

- The siddur's three-column tables (grain, wine, fruit) are set as indented
  alternatives, each with an English rubric, to fit the pocket page.
- The festival insertions are printed as full lines, one per festival.
- On Shabbat the siddur reads וְנַחֲמֵנוּ, noting וּרְצֵה וְהַחֲלִיצֵנוּ as an
  alternative; the alternative is given in the rubric.
- The English rubrics are our own wording, not the siddur's.
- As in the siddur, there is no Rosh HaShanah insertion.
