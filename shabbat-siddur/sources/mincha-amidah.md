## Editorial decisions

- Chazzan's repetition: Kedushah and Modim deRabbanan only, each under a
  rubric (Yosef). The silent Amidah is printed once.
- Seasonal insertions (Shabbat Shuvah, summer/winter, Rosh Hodesh and Hol
  HaMoed, Hanukkah and Purim) use the `seasonal` layout, as in Birkat HaMazon.
- **The Name in blessing closings:** the source points the Name in several
  closings with special vowels (a kabbalistic custom: all hiriq, all patah,
  etc., and once as a letter string with added vavs). Drafted with the
  standard pointing; to be decided by Yosef.
- Atah Echad: the source's [הוּא] kept in brackets with a rubric.

## Open (siddur photos)

  notes an expanded Isaiah 6 wording.
- **Atah Echad:** congregational Amen points in the repetition.

- "-ach" endings (תּוֹרָתָךְ etc.) as in the source; check against the siddur.
