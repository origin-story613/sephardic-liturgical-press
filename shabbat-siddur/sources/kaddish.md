# Kaddish

Section file: `sections/kaddish-half.tex`, `kaddish-shalem.tex`, `kaddish-yehei-shelama.tex`, `kaddish-al-yisrael.tex` (in `shabbat-siddur/`).

## Source

- **Text:** *Siddur Edot HaMizrach*, version "Torat Emet 357", as published by
  Sefaria (Sefaria-Export, October 2026): `Shabbat Mincha/Uva LeSion` 4 (the opening paragraphs, used in every form); `Shabbat Mincha/Amida` 41–43, 47; `Weekday Shacharit/Incense Offering` 32–34.
- **Original source:** Torat Emet freeware (toratemetfreeware.com).
- **License:** **Public Domain** (per Sefaria's version metadata).

## Editorial decisions

- Each Kaddish is one file, input wherever it is said, so it is printed in
  full every time (Yosef: all Kaddishes in full, with responses).
- Names: Half Kaddish; Kaddish Shalem (with תִּתְקַבַּל); Kaddish Yehei
  Shelama (the mourner's Kaddish); Kaddish Al Yisrael (after Rabbi Yishmael).
- The source's response marks `[אָמֵן]` and `[אָמֵן - יהא שמיה רבה ...]` become
  `\response{אָמֵן}`; the line יְהֵא שְׁמֵיהּ רַבָּא follows in full as in the source.
- Before the final עוֹשֶׂה שָׁלוֹם of Yehei Shelama and Al Yisrael, the
  source's note (take three steps back) is an English rubric.
