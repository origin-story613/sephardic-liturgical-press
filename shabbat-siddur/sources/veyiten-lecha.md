## Editorial decisions

- VeYiten Lecha only, no Motzei Shabbat songs (Yosef).
- The source's opening note (said every Motzei Shabbat, best said by two
  together) is an English rubric; its citation is dropped.
- וַאֲמַרְתֶּם כֹּה לֶחָי: the source says it seven times (some, once per member of
  the household); printed seven times in full.
- **Not included:** the further collections in the same source node
  (אֵלִיָּהוּ הַנָּבִיא 130 times, fourteen three-word verses, Ramban's ten verses,
  the nun verses, רִבּוֹנוֹ שֶׁל עוֹלָם, and others). Yosef to decide.
