# Havdalah

Section file: `shared/texts/havdalah.tex`, used by both Seder HaShulchan and the
Shabbat Siddur (moved from `seder-hashulchan/sections/` by Yosef's decision, so
both books share one text).

## Source

- **Text:** *Siddur Edot HaMizrach*, version "Torat Emet 357", as published by
  Sefaria (Sefaria-Export, September 2026): `Havdalah/Havdala`.
- **Original source:** Torat Emet freeware (toratemetfreeware.com).
- **License:** **Public Domain** (per Sefaria's version metadata).

Havdalah as its own section is Yosef's decision. **Compared with Yosef's
siddur (p. 324) and changed to follow it throughout (Yosef)**; see "Siddur
changes" below.

## Editorial decisions

- אָנָּא … הוֹשִׁיעָה נָּא and אָנָּא … הַצְלִיחָה נָא: printed once each under
  the rubric "Each line is said twice", as the siddur marks them "(2)".
- The spice blessing is set as alternatives, each with an English rubric (as in
  Me'ein Shalosh), in the siddur's order (מִינֵי, עֲצֵי, עִשְׂבֵּי); the rubrics
  carry the siddur's footnotes (cloves, myrtle, herbs).
- The blessing after wine is not repeated here; the rubric points to Me'ein
  Shalosh (shared text).
- **Not included** (Yosef): the source's "Before Havdalah" material (Eliyahu HaNavi, the בְּסִימָן טוֹב prayer, שַׁעֲרֵי אוֹרָה, and the
  prayers of R. Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev).

## Siddur changes (Yosef's siddur, p. 324)

- **Opening verses:** only רִאשׁוֹן לְצִיּוֹן … מְבַשֵּׂר אֶתֵּן. The source's
  other verses (אַל תִּשְׂמְחִי … , לַיְּהוּדִים … , וַיְהִי דָוִד … , וְנֹחַ … , כֵּן
  נִמְצָא …) and קוּמִי אוֹרִי are removed.
- **Rubrics** follow the siddur's instructions: lift the cup before רִאשׁוֹן
  לְצִיּוֹן; cup in the left hand and spices in the right; the fire blessing "by
  the light of a candle" (replacing the source's fingernails instruction).
- **הַצְלִיחֵנוּ:** הַצְלִיחַ דְּרָכֵנוּ, הַצְלִיחַ לִמּוּדֵנוּ (source: הַצְלַח
  דְּרָכֵינוּ, הַצְלַח לִמּוּדֵינוּ); ends כֵּן יִהְיֶה עִמָּנוּ [תָּמִיד] with the
  last word bracketed, explained by a rubric.
- **Savri Maranan:** סַבְרִי מָרָנָן (source: סַבְרֵי); response [לְ]חַיִּים.
- **Lemon:** הַנּוֹתֵן רֵיחַ טוֹב בַּפֵּרוֹת added.
- **Havdalah blessing:** לְחוֹשֶׁךְ (source: לְחֹשֶׁךְ).
- Kept, though not on the siddur's page: the rubric that spices and fire are
  omitted on Motzei Yom Tov, and the pointer to the blessing after wine.
